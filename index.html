For grown-ups

Farm Friends is a free game from Oak Hill Farm in Holmdel, New Jersey. Kids look after the farm's real animals, from Boots the Clydesdale and Gaby the alpaca to Rodney the rooster, through four seasons of farm days.

It plays in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. There's nothing to install and no account to make, and progress saves on the device you play on. Read-aloud starts off; tap the speaker button at the top right to turn it on.

Found a secret code in one of the coloring books? Type it in the game's Settings.

What's inside

  • 32 farm days of feeding, brushing, gardening and rounding up goats
  • Story games from the books: The Great Pumpkin Roll, Rodney Oversleeps and Peekaboo, George!
  • Every coloring page from the books, to color on screen or print
  • Two players on one keyboard, or with two game controllers
